Vendor fraud costs companies an estimated 5% of revenue a year, per the ACFE. Most of it isn't sophisticated — a changed bank account, a split purchase order, an invoice for goods that never arrived. VendorGuard catches all three automatically, inside Odoo, at the exact moment someone tries to pay it. Nine signals, real fraud-examiner and forensic-accounting techniques — not a black box. Let's show you.
